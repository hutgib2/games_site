## Description
Eat the oranges to increase your score but avoid touching the walls or yourself otherwise it's game over!


## How to Play
- WASD to move
- Eating oranges makes the snake grow and gives you a point
- Eating exotic fruit gives you a temporary powerup
- Each powerup only lasts 10 seconds

## Powerups
- Shrink: Sets snake to be the mininum size
- Multiplier: Doubles score gained from eating oranges
- Bounce: Snake deflects off walls
- Grow: Turns the small orange into a big pumpkin
- Ghost: Snake can move through its own body
- Slow: Slows down the snake for better control
- Portal: Snake teleports to the other side when touching wall
