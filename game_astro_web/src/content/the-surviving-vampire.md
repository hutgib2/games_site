## Description
Vampire Survivor is an endless game where you shoot bats, skeletons and demon bosses. Try to survive for as long as possible, while collecting fun powerups to help you stay alive. You have 3 lives before you die, the aim is to collect as many points as you can by killing enemies before the innevitable happens...


## How to Play
- Arrow keys/ASDW to move 
- Mouse to aim and shoot
- Spacebar to use ultimate move - does 1 damage to ever enemy on the screen

## Powerups
- **Extra Life**: Revives a lost life
- **Super-speed**: Increase movement speed x3
- **Slow zone**: Slows down enemies near the player
- **Spawn stop**: Temporarily pause the spawning of enemies
- **Shield**: Player temporarily unable to take damage
- **Rifle**: Bullets can pierce through enemies
- **Shotgun**: Sprays 5 bullets at a time
- **Machine gun**: Increased fire rate x3
- **Sideshot**: Fires bullets in all 4 directions
- **Lasergun**: Fires a laser bullet that spawns additional deadly lasers on contact with enemies
- **Flamegun**: Fires bullets that burn the ground on contact, temporarily creating a deadly fire in its place
- **Mine**: Player drops mines every second, which explode on contact
- **Sword**: Melee weapon that kills enemies on contact, except for the boss
