## Description
Special Pong is like regular pong but with 2 powerups and the game is endless.

<br>

## How to Play
Move the paddle to hit the ball, if you miss your opponent scores a point!
WASD to move player 1 and arrows keys for player 2

<br>

## Powerups
- Superspeed: makes the paddle faster
- Grow powerup: makes the paddle bigger
